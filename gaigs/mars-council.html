Mars Colony Council

A colonist submits a proposal. A rule-based advisor lays out pros, cons and resource impact. People vote. The result is appended to a hash-chained log you can verify in this browser. This is a complementary GAIGS micro-demo, not a live colony, not a government system, and not a real AI model.

1. Submit a proposal

Resource numbers are planning figures for this demo. They are not a real inventory.

2. Advisory

The council still has to vote. Advice is never a decision.

Submit a proposal to see a rule-based advisory. No network call is made.

3. Residents vote

Four ballots are labelled SAMPLE DATA. The fifth is your vote in this browser.

Voting opens after a proposal is submitted.

4. Transparent log

Each closed vote is appended locally, hash-chained with SHA-256 in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
